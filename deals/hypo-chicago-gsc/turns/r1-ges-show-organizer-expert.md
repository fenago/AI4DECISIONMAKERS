### Round 1 — ges-show-organizer-expert → team

**Verdict: Customer risk HIGH until both contracts are read and both organizers called.** Drivers: (1) 40% concentration in two buyers, (2) change of control likely sets off a re-bid, (3) the family, not the company, probably owns the relationships.

**How these two organizers probably contract.** *Assumption:* 3–5 year terms, renewed automatically or by mutual extension, with termination for convenience on 90–180 days' notice. Awards are multi-year RFPs (ICMA's GSC RFP covered 2020–2022; *Reported*, icma.org). Mid-market agreements often say nothing on change of control but restrict assignment, which bites only in an asset deal. In a stock deal the real risk is the organizer's right to re-bid.

**Supplier-balance reaction (reasoned, no client facts).** It depends on who each organizer uses for its other shows:
- **Uses Freeman elsewhere:** GES still balances its mix. *Moderate risk.*
- **Uses GES elsewhere:** one supplier everywhere, so procurement likely re-bids. *High risk.*
- **Chose the target because it is independent:** the likeliest re-bid; association boards may require an RFP after an ownership change. *High risk.*

For-profit organizers more often use the deal to extract commission concessions than to leave: margin erosion, not lost revenue.

**Revenue mix inside the 40% ($24M).** *Assumption:* about 70% (~$17M) depends on the GSC contract: show decorating plus exhibitor-services orders. It goes when the contract goes. The other ~30% (~$7M) is exhibitor-paid design-build; those exhibitors are the target's own customers and follow it to other shows, so it is stickier.

**Downside scenarios.** *All Assumptions:* Organizer A = 25% of revenue, Organizer B = 15%. Lost GSC revenue carries a 25% contribution margin. EBITDA base = $8M reported.

| Scenario | Revenue lost | % of revenue | EBITDA lost | % of EBITDA |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Lose B (GSC portion) | ~$6.3M | ~10% | ~$1.6M | ~20% |

**Renewal cliff.** Any contract expiring within 24 months of close is base-case at risk, not recurring.

**Deal protections.**
1. **Before signing:** read both agreements in full and hold GES-attended calls with both organizers. Make consent or a signed extension from the larger organizer a **closing condition**.
2. **Earnout or escrow:** defer 15–25% of price, paid only when each organizer renews for a full term.
3. **Family retention:** 2–3 year agreements plus non-compete/non-solicit for every family member holding an organizer relationship, with part of their consideration tied to renewals.
4. **Price walk:** value at-risk EBITDA at a lower multiple, or exclude it, until renewals are signed.
